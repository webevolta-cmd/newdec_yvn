### Facts deliberately not published

- **Prices.** Only dated promotional overlays exist (July–October 2026: 10 000 and 13 000 AMD for 3 mm
  sheets after a discount from about 16 000; 30 000 and 35 000 AMD for single panels; 18 000 AMD for
  an MDF 3D panel; 7 000–12 000 AMD for stone panels). Their attribution to products is partly
  inferred, so the site says the price depends on model and quantity.
- **Superlatives** ("largest assortment in Armenia", "most affordable prices") — single-source.
- **Warranty, delivery, installation service, certifications, fire rating** — never stated.
- **Interior design service** — the logo says "INTERIOR DESIGN" but no service is described.
- **Reviews and ratings** — none exist (Yandex: 0 reviews; no Google Maps listing found).
